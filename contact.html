Good to know

Before you call.

What should I bring to the first meeting?

Whatever you have: your plot details or site photos, a rough budget, and pictures of homes or rooms you like. Don't worry if you only have an idea. That's where we start.

Can you do just one part, like only the interiors?

Yes. You can come to us for architecture, construction, interiors or consultancy on its own, or let one team take care of everything from the first drawing to the keys.

Do you take on commercial projects too?

Yes. Along with homes, we design and build offices, shops and larger commercial and institutional buildings.

I'm not ready to build yet. Can I still talk to you?

Of course. Many projects begin with a simple question about a plot, a budget or a design. Our consultancy service is made for exactly that.
