The difference

Conventional homes vs a GS home.

In North India, even well-designed homes often fail because systems are not integrated and execution is inconsistent. Here is how we build differently.

Topic Conventional homes A GS home
Summer heat Extreme heat gain through walls and roofs Orientation, shading and insulation keep rooms cooler
Air-conditioning Heavy dependence on AC to feel liveable Ventilation and daylight designed in, so AC is needed less
Electricity bills Rising year after year Low-energy design, with energy estimates shared up front
Damp & seepage Common after a few monsoons Waterproofing planned with the structure, damp-free RCC foundations
Cracks & upkeep Grow over time Framed RCC structure in M25 concrete, with annual inspections
Systems Structure, services and finishes not integrated Structure, MEP and finishes planned together
Execution Inconsistent from site to site Standardised practices and quality checks on every site
Responsibility No one takes responsibility for performance Performance-linked turnkey with a 5–10 year warranty
