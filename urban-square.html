The project

A commercial street planned around people: shaded walkways, lively shopfronts and room to gather.

Urban Square brings shops, a hotel block and parking together around a landscaped pedestrian street.

Trees, planting and seating soften the space, so it works as a place to meet and spend time, not just to pass through.
