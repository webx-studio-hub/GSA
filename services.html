Design process & flow

Six steps behind every design.

A home should not depend on machines to feel liveable. It should be comfortable without excess energy, durable across generations and predictable to maintain.

  1. 01

    Site orientation & climate response

    We set the building to the sun path, prevailing winds and microclimate of your plot.

  2. 02

    Spatial zoning & massing

    Rooms are placed by privacy, views, access and how each space will be used.

  3. 03

    Natural ventilation & daylight

    Openings, courtyards and shading bring in light and moving air, so the home stays comfortable with less energy.

  4. 04

    Green & sustainable design

    Landscape, terraces and sustainable materials are part of the design from the first sketch.

  5. 05

    Structural system & durability

    We choose the right structural system for safety, a long life and good performance.

  6. 06

    Detailing & execution excellence

    Precise drawings and quality execution on site, for value that lasts.

Our philosophy

Beyond construction.

  1. 01

    50+ years of structural stability

    Every structure is a framed structure on columns, for the best finishes, a stable building and faster work on site.

  2. 02

    RCC foundations for earthquake safety

    High-grade M25 concrete in the foundations, built for seismic zone IV and a lifetime free of damp.

  3. 03

    Standardisation & good practice

    Windows sized for light, rain and wind; energy and water planned in; and an area chart before planning, so no space is wasted.

  4. 04

    Fault-free electricity

    Proper panels and boards on cable trays, dedicated shafts for high and low voltage, and conduits laid to drawings so faults are easy to trace.
