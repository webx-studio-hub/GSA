Who we are

We bring together creativity, precision and innovation to shape spaces that inspire, and we stay with you from the idea to the keys.

Established in 1999, GS Associates has been creating thoughtful, timeless and practical spaces that enrich the way people live, work and experience design. Over more than two decades we have delivered residences, commercial spaces, corporate offices, hospitality projects, hotels, interiors, palaces and turnkey developments.

Our design philosophy is rooted in sustainable architecture, where good looks meet function and care for the environment. We believe great architecture should not only inspire, but also be practical, efficient and built to stand the test of time.

Every project starts with a deep understanding of your vision, lifestyle and needs. From concept to execution, we deliver spaces that are innovative, functional and crafted with precision.

Our framework

25 years, one direction.

From a small architecture consultancy to dedicated commercial and residential divisions, every step has been about building better: thoughtful design, quality construction and timely delivery.

With over 25 years of experience in architecture and construction, our director brings rich design knowledge and a deep understanding of what clients need.

  1. 1999

    Founded by Gurpreet Singh as an architecture consultancy serving residential and industrial projects across Punjab.

  2. 2008

    Began turnkey residential projects, with one aim: to deliver quality homes to our clients.

  3. 2014

    Expanded our consultancy to large-scale and corporate projects, along with international sourcing.

  4. 2020

    Grew our construction work, known for quality and timely delivery, creating prominent landmarks.

  5. 2025

    Gave our commercial and residential work their own dedicated divisions.

Our strength

What makes us different.

  1. Hands-on approach

    Our principal architects and team personally oversee each project from concept to completion, so every detail is built with precision and quality. That care has earned us a name for excellence and reliability.

  2. Contemporary style

    We take inspiration from modern design and add practicality and creativity. Blending new ideas with timeless details, we create spaces that look striking and are easy to live in.

  3. Complete expertise

    Architecture, construction and interiors under one roof. Because one team designs and builds it all, the outside of your building and the rooms inside fit together perfectly.

  4. Client trust

    Consistent delivery, open communication and a personal approach. Every project is a partnership, and your vision stays at the heart of what we create.
